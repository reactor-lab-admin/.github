# 🚀 Reactor Lab

### Research. Develop. Deploy.

Reactor Lab is an interdisciplinary research and development laboratory in the Department of Civil, Construction and Environmental Engineering at Iowa State University, dedicated to building next-generation intelligent systems for smarter transportation, safer roads, and data-driven public infrastructure.

Our lab focuses on the fusion of Artificial Intelligence, Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, Computer Vision, Vision-Language Intelligence, and Real-Time Decision Systems to transform how roadway environments are monitored, analyzed, and optimized. From large-scale traffic data analytics to live AI-powered dashboards and deployable industrial products, Reactor Lab bridges advanced research with practical implementation.

---

## 🔬 Core Research Domains

- Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning
- Big Data Analytics for Transportation Systems
- Computer Vision & Real-Time Video Intelligence
- Vision-Language and Multimodal AI Models
- Smart Workzone and Traffic Monitoring Systems
- Cloud-Native AI Deployment (AWS • Oracle Cloud • GCP)
- Intelligent Dashboards and Decision Support Platforms
- Healthcare and Naturalistic Driving Behavior Analytics

---

## 🧠 What We Build

At Reactor Lab, we develop scalable cloud-based intelligent systems that can process large volumes of roadway, sensor, and video data in real time to support transportation agencies, industrial stakeholders, and public safety operations.

Our work includes:

- AI-powered traffic monitoring and roadway analytics
- Live dashboard platforms for transportation experts and decision-makers
- Smart workzone safety and congestion detection systems
- Multimodal AI pipelines for visual reasoning and contextual scene understanding
- Big data frameworks for connected vehicle and infrastructure analytics
- Cloud-hosted industrial products using AWS, Oracle Cloud, and Google Cloud Platform

We emphasize not only research publication, but also deployable end-to-end solutions that can operate in real-world environments.

---

## 🩺 Healthcare & Human-Centered Mobility Research

Beyond transportation intelligence, Reactor Lab is actively expanding into healthcare-driven mobility research — studying how neurological disorders, cognitive decline, chronic health conditions, and diabetic impairments influence naturalistic driving behavior, roadway interaction, and long-term safety outcomes.

By combining multimodal sensing, AI-driven behavior analysis, and clinical data interpretation, the lab aims to build intelligent systems that connect human health with safer mobility planning.

## 🤝 Collaborative Research Ecosystem

Reactor Lab is powered by PhD researchers and graduate students from multiple disciplines across Iowa State University, creating a strong interdisciplinary environment at the intersection of engineering, artificial intelligence, transportation, and health analytics.

The lab actively collaborates with:

- State and federal transportation agencies
- Industry partners and infrastructure stakeholders
- Cloud computing providers
- Cross-departmental academic researchers

This collaborative model allows us to develop solutions that are scientifically rigorous, industrially relevant, and socially impactful.

---

## 🌎 Our Vision

Our vision is to engineer intelligent infrastructure systems where roads, vehicles, data, and human behavior work together to create a safer, smarter, and more responsive future.

We believe the future of transportation lies not only in collecting data — but in building AI systems capable of understanding, reasoning, predicting, and acting on that data in real time.

Reactor Lab exists to make that future deployable.
